About Us

Pixel Quest Gaming Club is a friendly place for people who enjoy video games and meeting other players. Our club welcomes gamers of different skill levels.

Games

Members can play games together, discover new games, and meet other people who share their interest in gaming.

Events

We host weekly game nights and friendly tournaments where members can play, compete, and have fun together.

  • Weekly Game Nights — Join us for relaxed gaming sessions with other members.
  • Friendly Gaming Tournaments — Compete with other players in a fun and respectful environment.
  • New Player Meetups — Meet other gamers and learn about the club.

Community

Our community is welcoming to new and experienced players. Come meet people, share gaming tips, and make new friends.
